Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Harvest Root, Coorg, India

Welcome to Harvest Root. We are a family-owned spice venture based in Madikeri, Coorg, Karnataka. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share with us in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India and international privacy standards.

Our Core Promise: We collect only the information strictly required to deliver farm-fresh spices to your doorstep. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or place an order, we may collect the following details:

  • Contact & Delivery Information: Full name, shipping address, email address, and contact phone number.
  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, password hash (salted and encrypted), or profile information authorized via Google OAuth.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, quantity, transaction reference numbers, and order timestamps.
  • Support & Inquiries: Information you provide when contacting customer support or sending inquiry forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information solely for legitimate operational and business purposes:

  • To prepare, pack, and dispatch your spice orders from our Coorg plantations.
  • To send essential transactional notifications (OTP verification, order confirmations, and delivery updates).
  • To provide customer support and respond to spice inquiries.
  • To comply with statutory accounting and tax obligations.

3. Payment Security

All online payments are handled directly by PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (e.g., UPI, credit/debit cards, NetBanking). Harvest Root never stores or processes your complete credit card numbers, CVV codes, or UPI PINs on our servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We maintain a privacy-first approach to web storage:

  • Essential Session Cookies: Used solely to maintain your authentication state when logged into your account.
  • Local Storage: Used to temporarily persist your shopping cart items across visits so you do not lose your selections.
  • We do not load third-party ad tracking pixels or behavioural retargeting trackers.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We share your data only with trusted partners strictly necessary to fulfill your orders:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Delivery address and phone number for parcel fulfillment.
  • Transactional Communications: Verified email service providers to deliver OTP codes and order receipts.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You have the right to access, update, or request permanent deletion of your account and personal information at any time. To request account deletion:

Send an email to harvestroot2020@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", or submit a request directly through our Contact Form (select the Data Privacy option). We will verify and process your request within 7 business days.

7. Contact & Grievance Officer

If you have any questions, concerns, or grievances regarding this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us at:
