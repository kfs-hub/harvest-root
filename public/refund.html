Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Harvest Root, Coorg, India

At Harvest Root, we take pride in delivering farm-fresh, single-origin spices directly from our family plantations in Coorg. Your satisfaction and health are our top priorities. Please review our refund, return, and cancellation policies below.

1. Food Safety & Non-Returnable Items

Because whole spices and agricultural seasonings are consumable food products, packages that have been opened or unsealed cannot be returned or restocked under applicable food safety, hygiene, and FSSAI regulations.

Hygiene Exemption: Opened food products cannot be returned. However, if your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, we will replace or refund it immediately!

2. Damaged in Transit, Defective, or Incorrect Spices

If your package arrives compromised, we offer a 100% Free Replacement or Full Refund. Qualifying circumstances include:

  • Outer or inner airtight pouch was torn or damaged during transit.
  • Incorrect spice product or quantity delivered compared to your invoice.
  • Demonstrable quality defect upon opening an unexpired package.

3. How to Submit a Claim (48-Hour Window)

To ensure quick resolution, please follow these simple steps within 48 hours of package delivery:

  • Email us at harvestroot2020@gmail.com or WhatsApp our support line at +91 99013 65868.
  • Mention your Order ID and customer name.
  • Attach clear photographs or a short video showing the outer shipping label and the damaged/incorrect product.

Our team will review your claim and issue an approval or resolution within 24 business hours.

4. Order Cancellation Policy

You may cancel an order free of charge at any point prior to shipment dispatch from our Coorg estate. To cancel, please email us immediately with your Order ID.

Once a courier pickup has occurred and tracking has been generated, orders cannot be cancelled mid-transit. However, our damage replacement guarantee applies upon receipt.

5. Refund Method & Timeline

Once an approved refund is processed by our team:

  • Refunds are automatically issued back to your original payment method (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or NetBanking).
  • UPI Payments: Typically reflect in your account within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Card / NetBanking Payments: Typically reflect within 5 to 7 business days depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle.
