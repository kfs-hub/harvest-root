Our Story

Grown with Love in
the Heart of Coorg

Nestled in the misty hills of Coorg — India's spice paradise — Harvest Root is a third-generation family venture. Our plantations, shaded by silver oak and rosewood canopies, yield some of the world's finest spices.

Every peppercorn is sun-dried. Every clove is hand-sorted. We skip the middlemen and bring you spices that still carry the aroma of the Western Ghats — the way nature intended.
